Calibration sheet

Pages a device test rig drives with a real pointer and keyboard. Each page reports what it sees (the pointer, clicks, typed values, scrolling) so the rig measures instead of guessing.

  • calib.html: pointer gain and scroll step
  • lab.html: tap targets 8–60 px, a form, gestures, navigation
  • target.html: a second page to navigate to

Readings go to:

Add ?report=https://your-rig.example to any page to send them to your rig (an HTTPS address, from an HTTPS page).
